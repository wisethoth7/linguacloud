Children's Privacy Policy

LinguaCloud · Last updated: June 10, 2026 · Effective date: June 10, 2026

Plain Language Summary: LinguaCloud is a language learning app for children. We take children's privacy very seriously. We do not show ads to children, we do not sell children's data, and parents can delete all data at any time. This policy explains exactly what we collect and why.

1. Who This Policy Covers

This Children's Privacy Policy applies to children under 13 years of age who use LinguaCloud with parental consent. It supplements our general Privacy Policy.

LinguaCloud complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the FTC's 2025 COPPA Rule amendments, which took effect June 23, 2025.

2. What We Collect From Children

We collect the minimum information necessary to provide the LinguaCloud service:

  • First name only — to personalize lessons ("Good morning, Sofia!")
  • Age — to show age-appropriate content (ages 3–8 vs 8–12)
  • Language selected — to show the right lessons
  • Lesson progress — which lessons completed, scores, streaks
  • Voice/audio during practice — processed in real-time for pronunciation feedback. Audio is NOT stored after the session ends.

We do NOT collect: last name, email address from the child, location, photos, videos, or any contact information from the child directly.

3. Parental Consent

Before a child under 13 can use LinguaCloud, we require verifiable parental consent. This means:

  • A parent or legal guardian must create the account
  • We send a verification email to the parent's email address
  • The parent must click a confirmation link before the child's account is activated
  • A simple checkbox is not sufficient — we use email verification

4. How We Use Children's Data

We use children's data only to:

  • Provide language lessons tailored to the child's age and progress
  • Show parents their child's weekly progress reports
  • Improve the accuracy of our AI tutors for children

We do NOT use children's data for advertising, marketing to the child, or any purpose unrelated to the educational service.

5. Voice and Audio Data

LinguaCloud includes speaking practice where children speak aloud to practice pronunciation. Here is how we handle this data:

  • Voice input is processed in real-time to provide pronunciation feedback
  • Audio recordings are NOT permanently stored on our servers
  • Voice data is processed by our AI model and the temporary data is deleted within the session
  • No voiceprints or biometric voice profiles are created or stored
  • Parents are informed of voice data use during the consent process

6. Data Sharing

We do NOT share children's personal information with:

  • Advertisers or marketing companies
  • Social media platforms
  • Data brokers
  • Any third parties for commercial purposes

We may share data with our technical service providers (e.g., Supabase for database, Vercel for hosting) solely to operate the service, under strict data processing agreements.

7. Data Retention

We retain children's data only as long as needed to provide the service:

  • Active account data: retained while account is active
  • Voice/audio: deleted within the session (not retained)
  • Upon account deletion: all children's personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days
  • We do not retain children's data after account deletion for marketing or analytics purposes

8. Parental Rights

As a parent or legal guardian, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of all personal information we have collected about your child
  • Delete — request deletion of all personal information about your child
  • Correct — request correction of any inaccurate information
  • Withdraw consent — withdraw your consent at any time, which will deactivate the child's account
  • Opt out of future collection — while still allowing use of the service (where technically feasible)

To exercise any of these rights, email us at: privacy@linguacloud.app with "COPPA Request" in the subject line. We will respond within 10 business days.

9. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all data transmission
  • Encrypted database storage
  • Limited employee access to children's data (need-to-know basis only)
  • Regular security reviews

10. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to how we handle children's personal information, we will notify parents by email before the changes take effect and obtain renewed parental consent where required by law.

11. Contact Us

For questions about this policy or to exercise parental rights:

  • Email: privacy@linguacloud.app
  • Subject line: "COPPA Request — [Your Child's First Name]"
  • Response time: Within 10 business days
