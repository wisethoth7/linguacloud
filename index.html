CHILD SAFETY

Built for children.
Safe by design.

We take children's safety seriously. Every design decision puts your child's wellbeing first.

Zero Ads. Ever.
No advertising, no sponsored content, no tracking pixels. LinguaCloud is subscription-only so your child's attention belongs to their learning, not advertisers.

All content is reviewed and rated for each age tier (3–8 and 8–14). The AI is specifically constrained to educational, age-appropriate conversation only.
